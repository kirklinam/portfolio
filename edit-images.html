🖼️ Portfolio Image Editor

Drag the dot on any photo to choose what stays centered. Switch Fill (crop to fill the frame) or Whole (show the entire photo, no cropping). Change the frame Shape or Zoom as needed. When you're happy, click Download CSS and drop the file into your site folder (replace the existing image-adjustments.css), then refresh the site.

Tip: photos that get cut off at the edges usually look best on Whole. Photos where a face/subject is off to one side look best on Fill with the dot dragged onto the subject. Your changes auto-save in this browser; the downloaded CSS is what actually updates the site.
